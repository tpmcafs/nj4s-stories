How to play

The whole class is one player: the coral ▶. The other side is the Victimitis virus.

  1. Pick a roller: a volunteer or a name stick. Passing is always OK.
  2. Read the square. Everyone answers on their Remote Paddle: green = proactive, red = reactive.
  3. 80% of paddles right? Keep rolling. Under 80%? Victimitis grows one bar.
  4. LEVEL UP (green): beat the challenge to climb the ladder.
  5. RAGE QUIT (red): Victimitis attacks. Anyone can rewrite the excuse. A good rewrite blocks the slide and heals one bar.
  6. PAUSE & CHOOSE: everyone pushes pause and counts 1-2-3 before showing paddles.
