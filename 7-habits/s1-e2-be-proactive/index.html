NEW WORD UNLOCKEDAdded to your vocabulary

PROACTIVE

Taking action BEFORE something happens, to make things better or stop a problem, instead of waiting and reacting after.

WORD CLUE: PRO-

PRO means ahead. You get ahead of the problem.

IN REAL LIFE

Test on Friday? You start studying on Monday.

LIKE THE WATER

Shake it all you want. Open it. Calm, cool, in control.

+1
NEW WORD UNLOCKEDAdded to your vocabulary

REACTIVE

Waiting until AFTER something happens, then responding to it, instead of acting first to stop it.

WORD CLUE: RE-

RE means back. Something happens, and you react back.

IN REAL LIFE

Test on Friday? You cram Thursday night, then blame the teacher.

LIKE THE SODA

Shake it up and the pressure builds… then it explodes.

Be Proactive

This habit is the key to all the other habits! Being proactive is about being in control.

PROACTIVE

Take responsibility for their life and MAKE IT HAPPEN.

REACTIVE

Blame others for their problems and GET HAPPENED TO.

The choice is yours.
