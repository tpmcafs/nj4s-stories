A story from the book · two readers

Too busy sawing

You come upon a guy in the woods, furiously sawing down a tree.

YOU“How long have you been at it?”

THE SAWYER“Four hours, but I’m making progress.”

YOU“Your saw looks really dull. Why don’t you take a break and sharpen it?”

THE SAWYER“I can’t, you idiot. I’m too busy sawing.”

Who’s the real idiot? Have you ever been too busy living to take time to renew yourself?

Heart · caring for your heart

Laugh or you’ll cry

Laughter loosens your mental gears, helps you cope and connects you with others.

  • Keep promises to yourself and others
  • Small acts of kindness · be loyal and honest
  • Listen · say you’re sorry · set clear expectations

Feeling down most days for weeks? Don’t handle it alone. Talk to a parent, a counselor, a teacher or another adult you trust. Asking for help is a proactive choice.
