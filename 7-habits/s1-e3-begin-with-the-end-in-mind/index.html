    How to begin with the end in mind

    Your personal mission statement

    • States what your life is about: the blueprint (or constitution) of your future
    • Can be very personal
    • Can be written, or expressed through art, dance, music, a talent
    • Will change over time, just like you
    • Will open your eyes to what matters most
    • Will help you make decisions accordingly
    Pick your genre

    4 ways to write your mission statement

    The quote collectionCollect your favorite quotes. Together they become your mission statement.
    The brain dumpBrainstorm nonstop for 5 min (use your Great Discovery). Then 20 min to edit.
    The retreatPlan a big chunk of time in a quiet spot. No distractions. Think deeply.
    The big lazyAny form. One word, ten, a paragraph, as long as it means something to you.
    Speed rounds · optional

    3 watch-outs on the road

    1Negative labels“Lazy.” “Trouble.” “Not a math person.”You are not your labels. Don’t let other people’s pre-judgments limit you.
    2“It’s all over”“I messed up. Who cares now?”It’s never over. Every comeback story you just heard started with a setback.
    3The wrong wallClimbing fast, in the wrong direction.It doesn’t matter how fast you climb if your ladder is on the wrong wall. Remember Thanos?
