Use an agenda

An agenda wasn’t designed to tie you down. It was designed to FREE you up.

  • Never forget when papers are due, tests are scheduled or when you work
  • Keep phone numbers and anything else important
  • An agenda is just a tool to help you be more effective
  • Planning is what gives the “picture” a chance to become real
