WHAT IS CULTURE?
What does culture mean to you?

Culture is the way people live. It includes the things that make a group of people unique and special.

🍲Food🗣️Language🎉Holidays🎶Music👕Clothes👪Family traditions
IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Culture facts

🌍Everyone has a culture
👥You can belong to more than one culture
⚖️No culture is better than another. Just different
🌐
EVERYONE
WHAT IS CULTURAL RESPECT?
Being kind to people who live, dress, speak, or believe differently.

It means understanding that not everyone is the same, and that’s okay.
