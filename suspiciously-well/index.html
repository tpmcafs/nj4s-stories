    Level Up or Burn Out rules

    • The whole room is one player: the red ▶. The other side is Burnout, five bars.
    • Self-talk, profile and choice squares: thumbs up = builds wellness, thumbs down = drains it. Under 80% right adds a bar.
    • Level Up (ladders): a challenge for a volunteer or the whole room. Beat it to climb.
    • Burnout slides (chutes): read the excuse, then anyone rewrites it as an “I will.” A good one blocks the slide and heals a bar.
    • Healing Burnout: 3 good rounds in a row, a blocked slide, or a right answer in a bonus round (squares 25, 50, 75) each take a bar away. No points, money, timers or lifelines to track.
    • The squares live in ladders.html, in the EDIT HERE block. Each one has a dimension.

    What each activity reinforces

    • Definitions: wellness is a deliberate process, not being happy all the time.
    • Stress sort: naming stress triggers and how we know we’re stressed.
    • Domino run and Slips: the eight dimensions are connected. A slip in one affects the others, and so does a good habit.
    • Check in: your Personal Assessment, 5 statements per dimension.
    • Cards and “I will”: pick an activity for your lowest dimension and commit to it (the peer coaching model).

    01 · What is wellness

    Two definitions. Only one is real.

    NopeA

    Happy all the time.

    Appearing happy to everyone all of the time. Never feeling stressed out or unhappy. Never having to ask for help, and having everything under control on your own.

    The real oneB

    A deliberate process.

    A conscious, deliberate process that requires a person to become aware of, and make choices for, a more satisfying lifestyle.

    Which one is more realistic, and why?

    Definition B, the realistic one

    Wellness is a process.

    Nobody feels great every day. Wellness is noticing how you're doing, then making choices that move you toward a more satisfying life.

      Questions from the room

      Anything typed into the question bar on the first screen lands here, with no names attached, so you can answer it before you close.

        Do I have to be great at all eight?

        Nope. Nobody is. Most people have a couple that are strong and a couple that need work. The goal is to notice, then nudge.

        What if my lowest one is something I can't control?

        Some things, like money at home, aren't yours to fix. Pick the part you can touch: a habit, a conversation, or asking someone for help.

        Isn't asking for help kind of weird?

        It's one of the signs of doing well. Support made the list for a reason.

        How long until a new habit sticks?

        Longer than a week, shorter than you'd think. Research suggests it takes about two months on average, and missing one day doesn't reset you.

        Who can I talk to?

        A school counselor, a teacher you trust, a family member, or the NJ4S team. If things ever feel like too much, you can call or text 988 any time, day or night.

        What's with the glowing orb?

        It's you. Well, a version of you. It gets rounder when your eight dimensions are in balance and lumpier when one is low. Tinashe built it, and hid eight secrets on this page, one for each dimension. Tap the orb eight times to find your first.
