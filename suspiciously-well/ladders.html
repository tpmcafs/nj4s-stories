How to play

The whole room is one player: the red ▶. The other side is Burnout.

  1. Pick a roller: a volunteer or a name stick. Passing is always OK.
  2. Read the square. Everyone votes with a thumb: up = builds wellness, down = drains it.
  3. 80% of thumbs right? Keep rolling. Under 80%? Burnout grows one bar.
  4. LEVEL UP (green): beat the challenge to climb the ladder.
  5. BURNOUT SLIDE (red): an excuse drags you down. Anyone can rewrite it as an “I will” (what and when). A good rewrite blocks the slide and heals one bar.
  6. PAUSE & CHOOSE: everyone takes one slow breath before voting.
  7. The dot on each square shows which dimension it’s about.
