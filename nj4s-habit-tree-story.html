Why a tree

Built from the inside out.

The 7 Habits of Highly Effective Teens Sean Covey Personal Bank Account Session 1 callback NJ4S Bergen Hub Season 1 · Thursdays

A tree only looks like it starts at the ground. The real work happens underneath, in the roots, where nobody can see it. That is where Habits 1, 2 and 3 live: taking charge of yourself, knowing where you are going, and doing the big things first. Only then can a trunk carry weight, and only then can branches reach other people. Habit 7 is the ring of light around all of it. Stop watering a tree and it stops growing, no matter how strong its roots are.

Roots · Habits 1–3

Private Victory

Promises you keep to yourself. Deposits in the Personal Bank Account that nobody else sees.

Branches · Habits 4–6

Public Victory

Wins with other people. Trust, listening and ideas that need more than one person.

Ring · Habit 7

Renewal

Body, brain, heart and soul. The habit that keeps the other six alive.
